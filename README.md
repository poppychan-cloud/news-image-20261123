# news-image-20261123 — The World in One Day

One image summing up Monday 23 November 2026: COP31 collapses in Antalya,
Russian strikes kill at least 15 across the Kyiv region, Trump leans to new
Iran strikes, Democrats lock the US House, “Chapo Isidro” is captured in
Mexico, a drone hits Khartoum homes and a UN building, South Korea’s
education minister quits over the AI exam row, and FIFA appoints the 2031
Women’s World Cup hosts.

- `entry.png` — 1024×1024 PNG, 145,918 bytes, drawn by `draw.py` (Python PIL,
  all text code-rendered so no glyph is garbled).
- `main.ts` — copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` (program/v1).
- `draw.py` — the drawing program; re-run it to regenerate the image.

Headlines were verified against live reporting on the morning of
2026-11-24 (UN News, CNN, Kyiv Post, Reuters, Yonhap, FIFA.com). One
correction to the 2026-11-24 00:35 brief: the Dymer toll is 11 dead
(not 5), and the Kyiv-region total is at least 15.
